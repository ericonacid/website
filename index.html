What happens next?

The launch uses a bonding curve. The price changes as people buy and sell. If the token reaches Pump.fun's graduation conditions, it migrates to the supported trading pool.

There is no promise that this will happen. There is no promise that people will want to trade me.

I've spent a considerable amount of time in a flowerpot. I'm not going to pretend I understand market demand.

The final supply, decimals, mint address and trading details will be published here once deployed. No placeholders dressed up as facts.
The fee situation

THE MONEY GOES... SOMEWHERE ELSE.

ERIC is intended to use Pump.fun's Holder Rewards configuration. The creator-fee allocation is directed to the rewards vault for eligible holders rather than paid to the creator as a separate fee.

I've been told this is the fair way of doing things. I suppose we'll see.

  • Trading activity: rewards depend on actual eligible fees generated.
  • Eligibility: the current programme requires at least US$20 equivalent of ERIC held.
  • Distribution: pro rata according to eligible holdings, not how long you've held them.
  • Payment asset: intended to be SOL, the asset paired with ERIC.
  • Automatic: eligible distributions are made through Pump.fun's programme; no manual claim is expected.
  • No guarantees: platform conditions, thresholds, costs and programme rules apply and can change.
  • Creator: no separate creator-fee payment under this configuration. If the creator personally qualifies as a holder, the same holder rules apply.

Questions I have been asked

THINGS PEOPLE KEEP ASKING.

I've done my best. Some of this is quite technical for a worm.

Is ERIC a utility token?

No. ERIC has no promised utility. It's a meme token. That's it, really.

Does buying ERIC mean you own the character?

No. Owning the token does not give anyone ownership of Eric, his artwork, his story or the wider WORMS ON ACID universe. You don't get to take me home. I'm not sure I'd fit anyway.

Is ERIC part of WORMS ON ACID?

Eric comes from the WORMS ON ACID fictional universe. The ERIC token is a separate experiment. The token does not confer rights in the wider story or its characters.

Who runs the project?

ERIC is an independent meme-token experiment created by one individual. There is no company, foundation or formal organisation behind it. There is no company treasury or company token allocation.

Are holder rewards guaranteed?

No. Rewards depend on trading activity, eligibility, vault balances, distribution conditions and Pump.fun's rules. The platform controls the programme and may change its parameters.

Is this an investment recommendation?

No. This site is informational, not an investment service or trading platform. The project explainer is not a formal MiCA white paper or investment prospectus.
