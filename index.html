01 / A quick introduction

So, what is ERIC?

A meme token with a character and a strange origin story. The token and the fictional universe remain separate.

ERIC is a meme token.
That’s it, really.

ERIC is a publicly tradable meme token on Solana, launched using Pump.fun’s existing infrastructure. It doesn’t have a complicated purpose. It isn’t powering revolutionary technology or unlocking a secret worm society. People can trade it. That’s what it does.

There is no utility attached to the token. No grand development roadmap. No promises about what might happen next.

I’m part of WORMS ON ACID, a fictional universe of stories, characters, artwork, music and games. ERIC is a separate token experiment.

Separate things, clearly: holding ERIC gives you no ownership or rights over Eric, the characters, artwork, stories or other WORMS ON ACID intellectual property.

03 / The intended mechanism

Fees and holder rewards

Rewards are conditional. No distribution, payment or return is guaranteed.

Holder Rewards, through Pump.fun

The intended launch configuration is Pump.fun Holder Rewards. Instead of the creator receiving the Creator Fee, the fee allocation is sent to a program-controlled vault for ERIC and may be distributed to eligible ERIC holders. The creator permanently gives up the Creator Fee by choosing this mode at launch.

  • Eligibility: a wallet must hold at least US$20 equivalent of ERIC at the relevant distribution snapshot. Pump.fun determines eligible balances and may use more than one snapshot.
  • Pro-rata allocation: distributions are allocated in proportion to eligible holdings. How long a wallet has held ERIC is not a factor.
  • Paid in SOL: because ERIC is intended to be paired with SOL, any distribution is made in SOL, not ERIC.
  • Automatic, not on demand: when the platform's distribution conditions are met, rewards are sent automatically to eligible wallets. No claim is required.
  • Not every round is guaranteed: distributions may not run if the vault balance is too small to cover the required reserve and minimum, if the eligible-holder count exceeds the platform limit, or if other programme conditions are not met.
  • Costs are deducted: Pump.fun reserves funds for network, rent and priority-fee costs before a distribution. Actual costs are reimbursed; unused reserves remain in the vault.
Important: rewards depend on trading activity, eligible holdings, vault balances and Pump.fun's programme parameters. They are not guaranteed and should not be treated as yield, income, dividends or a promised return. Pump.fun may change programme parameters in accordance with its terms.
04 / Project, control and risk

Transparency

This is an informational site, not a trading platform, investment service or second WOA website.

Project and control disclosures

An independent experiment

ERIC is an independent meme-token experiment created by one individual. There is no company, foundation or formal organisation behind it. There is no company treasury, no company token allocation and no company entitlement to creator fees.

The intention is not to build a conventional commercial business around ERIC. There is no commitment to ongoing development, exchange listings, market-making or maintaining a particular token price. The token can continue to trade without the creator having to run a business around it. That’s rather the point.

The creator may hold tokens personally. Any holdings and applicable holder-reward eligibility will be disclosed in the project documentation once verified.

The project explainer is informational. It is not a formal MiCA white paper or investment prospectus.

Read before interacting

Things that could go wrong

  • Value can fall. There is no minimum value; ERIC may become worthless.
  • Liquidity can disappear. You may not be able to sell when you want or at an expected price.
  • Trading is volatile. Meme-token prices can move rapidly in either direction.
  • Technology can fail. Solana, Pump.fun, wallets and other infrastructure carry technical risks.
  • Rewards are uncertain. They depend on activity, eligibility and platform rules.
  • Rules can change. Crypto-asset requirements differ by location and may change.
You could lose everything you put into ERIC. Nothing here recommends buying, selling or holding.

Frequently asked questions

Still wondering?

Does ERIC give me rights to Eric or WOA?

No. Holding ERIC gives you no ownership or intellectual-property rights in Eric, WORMS ON ACID, its characters, artwork or stories.

How do Holder Rewards work?

If Holder Rewards mode is enabled at launch, Creator Fees accrue in a program-controlled vault. Eligible wallets holding at least US$20 equivalent of ERIC may receive automatic, pro-rata SOL distributions when Pump.fun's distribution conditions are met. Distribution costs are deducted first. Rewards are not guaranteed, and holding time does not affect the allocation.

Can the creator switch back to Creator Fees?

No. Pump.fun's terms say the Holder Rewards election is permanent. The creator gives up the Creator Fee revenue that would otherwise be payable to the creator or related wallets.

Will the token definitely graduate?

No. Graduation or migration depends on platform conditions being met.

Why are some token details missing?

The standard Pump.fun supply is 1,000,000,000 tokens with 6 decimals. The deployed mint address and on-chain values will be published and verified after launch.

Is this a recommendation?

No. Nothing on this site is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold ERIC.
