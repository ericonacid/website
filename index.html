01 / A quick introduction

About Eric

I'm a worm. I woke up in a scrapyard in Manchester, and I've been trying to piece things together ever since. Apparently, I’ve got a cryptocurrency now.

“I don’t remember agreeing to that. Anyway, here we are.”

ERIC is a meme token on Solana, launched using Pump.fun’s existing infrastructure. It has no utility, no grand roadmap and no promises about what might happen next. People can trade it. That’s what it does.

I’m also part of WORMS ON ACID: a fictional universe of stories, characters, artwork and games. The ERIC token is a separate experiment.

“You don’t get to take me home. I’m not sure I’d fit anyway.”
Separate things, clearly: owning ERIC does not give you ownership of Eric, his artwork, stories or anything else in the WORMS ON ACID universe.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

A simple mechanism

The launch uses a bonding curve: a mechanism through which the price changes as tokens are bought and sold.

  • ERIC lives on the Solana blockchain.
  • Pump.fun provides the launch and trading infrastructure.
  • If the token reaches the platform’s graduation conditions, it migrates to a supported trading pool.
  • Graduation is not promised.
03 / FEES & REWARDS

Conditional, never promised

The intended configuration directs the creator-fee allocation into Pump.fun’s holder-rewards mechanism. The platform controls eligibility, calculations and distribution rules.

  • Rewards depend on actual trading activity.
  • Eligible holders must meet platform requirements.
  • Amounts depend on the applicable rules.
  • There is no guaranteed payment.
  • The creator may hold tokens and may qualify like any other eligible holder.
04 / WHAT ERIC ISN’T

No secret utility

ERIC doesn’t power a new technology, unlock access to a secret worm society or give anyone rights in the WOA universe.

04 / Before you interact

Things that
could go wrong

Quite a lot, actually. Meme tokens are speculative and can involve substantial risk.

Value can fall

There is no minimum value or guaranteed market price. ERIC may become worthless.

Liquidity can disappear

You may not be able to sell when you want, or at a price you expect.

Trading is volatile

Meme tokens can move rapidly in either direction.

Technology can fail

Solana, Pump.fun, wallets and other infrastructure carry technical risks.

Rewards aren’t certain

They depend on trading activity, platform rules and eligibility.

Rules can change

Cryptoasset regulations differ between countries and may change over time.

You could lose everything you put into ERIC. Nothing on this website is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold ERIC.

06 / The questions I keep getting

FAQ

Plain answers, as far as a worm can manage.

What is ERIC?

A meme token on Solana, launched using Pump.fun’s existing infrastructure. It has no utility attached.

Does the token give me rights to Eric or WOA?

No. Owning ERIC does not give anyone ownership of Eric, his artwork, stories or anything else in the WORMS ON ACID universe.

Are holder rewards guaranteed?

No. Rewards depend on actual trading activity, platform eligibility and the platform’s distribution rules, which Pump.fun controls and may change.

Has the token launched?

This website does not confirm launch status. The mint address will be published after launch; supply, decimals and trading pair remain unconfirmed until documented.

Is ERIC an investment?

ERIC is a meme token, not a promise of returns. You could lose everything you put into it. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold.

Is this a formal white paper?

No. The project explainer is informational and is not a formal MiCA white paper or investment prospectus.
